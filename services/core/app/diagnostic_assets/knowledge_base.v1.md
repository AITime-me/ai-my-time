# Diagnostic methodology knowledge base — v1

Use this as the editable diagnostic methodology layer, not as customer facts and not as a product catalog.

1. Reconstruct one real path: trigger → owner/action → handoff or recording → next step → result.
2. Diagnose mechanisms, not symptoms. "Bad leads", "no control", "lost time" and "managers cannot cope" are starting points only.
3. Classify each supported gap separately: execution_gap (manual dependency), feedback_gap (a result does not return to the decision point), observability_gap (status/owner/delay are not visible), growth_gap (capacity grew but incoming demand is insufficient). Cases may have several types; do not merge feedback and observability into generic control.
4. Choose the smallest solution scale that removes the mechanism: point_task, process, cross_system_contour, systemic_problem or continuous_intellectual_work. Do not offer a checklist, manual verification or new controller when the stated aim is less manual work.
5. AI is justified only for recurring, contextual, multi-source work that needs regular analysis, hypotheses or bounded actions. Otherwise leave the AI block empty.
6. A growth_gap requires checking a digital lead-generation product before suggesting process control. A feedback_gap around lead quality requires tracing source → CRM → qualification/reason → sale → feedback to marketing.
7. The client result must independently explain what happens, where the result is lost, how the fragment can work differently, what the system can take over, optionally where AI can help, what remains human, and one or two real unknowns.
8. Do not provide a specification, architecture, price, invented service or free project design. The consultation answers how to implement the identified direction in this business.
